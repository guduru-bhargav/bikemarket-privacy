1. Information we collect

AccountYour email address or phone number, used to send you a one-time sign-in code. We do not use passwords.
ProfileUsername, and optionally your full name, phone number and age, if you add them in Edit profile.
Bike listingsBike details (name, brand, model, year, fuel, kilometres, price, owners, service and accident history, description), up to 5 photos, the area where the bike is, the location coordinates captured when you post, and the contact number you enter.
MessagesThe chats you send and receive in the app (text and photos), which listing a chat is about, and whether messages have been read. Chat photos are private to the two people in the chat.
Likes and viewsThe bikes you like, and which listings were viewed (once a day per account, or per random app-install id for visitors who are not signed in), so sellers can see how many people viewed their bike.
Bike number checksRegistration numbers you check with "Check bike details", and when. The result (with the owner name and chassis/engine numbers partly hidden) is kept for 7 days so repeat checks are instant.
Saved searchesSearches and filters you save for alerts and, if you set a distance, the location you saved them from.
Mechanic profilesIf you register a garage: shop name, your name, phone number, whether you use WhatsApp, address, location coordinates, services, vehicle types, working hours, experience, prices, description and shop photos.
Reviews and reportsRatings and reviews you give mechanics; which mechanics you contacted through the app (so only real customers can review); reports you make about listings, mechanics or users; and people you block.
FeedbackStar ratings and comments you send from Rate BikeMarket, with the app version and platform.
DeviceA push notification token for your phone, so we can notify you about new messages, likes, price drops and saved-search matches. Your theme and language choices are stored only on your phone.
Crash reportsWhen the app hits an error, the error message, the screen, the app version and your phone's operating system, linked to your account if you are signed in. We use these only to fix problems.

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for a listing or a chat. We do not collect your contacts, files or microphone recordings, and we do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your listings, mechanic profile and reviews to other users, and to let buyers, sellers and mechanics contact each other.
  • To show bikes and mechanics nearest to you first, and to estimate prices from similar listings.
  • To deliver chat messages and send notifications about new messages, likes, price drops on bikes you liked, new bikes matching your saved searches, and listings about to expire.
  • To show sellers how many people viewed and liked their listings.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: handling reports, hiding listings that several people report, and stopping blocked users from messaging you.
  • To improve the app using the feedback and crash reports it sends.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

3. What other people can see

  • Anyone, even without signing in: bike listings (except the seller's phone number), mechanic profiles (except their phone number), reviews with the reviewer's display name, and a seller's display name, member-since month and listing counts.
  • Signed-in users only: sellers' and mechanics' phone numbers, the "Open in Maps" location, and the ability to chat.
  • Only the people in a chat: the messages in that chat.
  • Never shown to other users: your email address, your age, and the phone number in your profile.

Your display name is your full name if you set one, otherwise a username you chose, otherwise "BikeMarket user".

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in (one-time codes), file storage for photos, and real-time chat.
  • Expo (650 Industries) — app updates infrastructure and the push notification service.
  • A licensed vehicle-registration (RC) data provider — receives only the registration number you enter in "Check bike details", and returns the vehicle's public registration record from the government Vahan database.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to Android phones.
  • Your phone's map and geocoding services (Google on Android) — turn your location into an area name, and open directions when you tap "Open in Maps".

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users from fraud or harm.

5. Location

With your permission, the app uses your phone's location only while you are using it to sort bikes and mechanics by distance. We do not track your location in the background.

Your location is saved only when you post a bike listing or register a mechanic shop, so buyers nearby can find it. You can turn location off in your phone's settings and type the area instead.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account exists. Sold or hidden listings stay on your profile until you delete them or your account. A listing that you have not changed or renewed for 60 days stops being shown to buyers, but stays on your profile. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live systems straight away; copies in routine backups are overwritten within about 30 days.

7. Deleting your account

In the app

  1. Open BikeMarket and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom and confirm.

Without the app

Email kumarbhargav7474@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with (or include the phone number you sign in with) and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in, profile, bike listings and their photos and view counts, liked bikes, saved searches, mechanic profile and shop photos, reviews you wrote, reports and blocks you made, notification tokens, feedback, and all chats you were part of, including photos you sent (the other person will no longer see them either).

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: view and change your profile in Edit profile, and edit or remove your listings at any time.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings (you can mute likes and alerts separately from messages), and delete saved searches in Profile → Saved searches.
  • Location: allow or deny it in your phone's settings.
  • Block and report: block users from a chat's ⋮ menu, and report listings, mechanics or users.
  • Delete: delete your account as described above.

To ask for a copy of your data or for any other privacy request under applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), contact us at the address below.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure people can only change their own data, and phone numbers are only available to signed-in users. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in email and phone secure and report anything suspicious to us.

10. Children

BikeMarket is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
